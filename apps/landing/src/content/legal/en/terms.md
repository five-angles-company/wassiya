## What Wassiya is, and what it is not

Wassiya is an encrypted vault where you keep your digital assets, information and messages, and choose what reaches each heir after your death.

- **Wassiya is not a legal authority and does not divide estates.** Shares are set by law under the fara'id, not by the app. We deliver what you routed by name, as whole items, and nothing more.
- **Wassiya does not replace a notarised will**, or any legal process that transfers ownership. What we deliver is access to information, not title.
- We do not give religious, legal or financial advice.

## Delivery after death

- **Every heir is silent:** we contact no heir before release.
- Delivery starts with a death report, which anyone may file. Filing a report gives the filer nothing.
- Release happens only after an official death certificate whose name our team matches to your verified legal name, followed by the end of the veto window.
- **You can stop any report with your fingerprint during the veto window**, and its filer is then barred from filing again for 90 days.
- After release we contact each heir on the number you registered, and deliver only once their identity is verified. If their identity does not match what you registered, our team decides after review, and we may refuse delivery if we cannot verify them.
- Each heir receives only what you routed to them. A delivery stays available for **one year** from release, then is destroyed for good.
- Anything you routed to no one is delivered to no one.
