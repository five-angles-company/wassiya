## What Wassiya is, and what it is not

- **Wassiya is not a legal authority and does not divide estates.** Shares are set by law under the fara'id, not by the app. We hand over what you marked to be handed over, as whole items, to the executors you named, and nothing more. Carrying out your will is theirs.

- **If you lose both your phone and your recovery sheet, nobody can open your vault — us included.**

- We cannot check or update what you store.

## Delivery after death

- **Executors hear nothing from us before release:** no invitation and no notification.
- Delivery starts with a death report, which anyone may file. The filer is never asked to verify their identity, and filing gives them nothing.
- **Missed check-ins release nothing.** Only a verified death report can.
- Release happens only after an official death certificate whose name our team matches to your verified legal name, followed by the end of the veto window.
- **You can stop any report with your fingerprint during the veto window**, and its filer is then barred from filing again for 90 days.
- After release we contact each executor on the number you registered. They must verify their identity, and their document must match the ID number you registered for them. If it does not, our team decides after review, and we may refuse delivery if we cannot verify them.
- The executor opens what you handed over with their executor sheet, or with your recovery sheet, in their own browser. Without either, we cannot open it for them.
- From release, your recovery sheet no longer restores your vault and no new device can be added to it; it opens only what you handed over, for a verified executor.
- Every executor receives everything you marked to be handed over. A delivery stays available for **one year** from release; then your whole vault is deleted for good.
- Anything you kept private is handed over to no one.
