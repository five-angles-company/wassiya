## Who we are

This policy covers the Wassiya app, wassiya.app, the web app for death reports and heirs, and support conversations.

## The principle: your vault is not ours

Your vault's contents are encrypted on your phone before they reach us, and we do not hold the key, so we cannot read them. What follows is what we **can** read, and why we need it. The encryption is explained in [How the encryption works](/en/legal/encryption).

## What we collect

### If you own a vault

- **Your account:** your name, email address, country and language. You sign in with a one-time code; there is no password.
- **Your identity check:** done through a specialist provider (Didit). From the result we keep your verified name, document type, date of birth and verification status. **We keep neither images of your document nor your ID number** — only a keyed, irreversible fingerprint of the number, to match against later.
- **Your vault:** encrypted content we cannot read, plus unencrypted metadata we need to run the service: each item's type, item count, size, file type, and a reminder date if you set one.
- **Your heirs:** the name, relationship, phone number, email and date of birth you register for each heir, and a fingerprint of their ID number if you enter it. We need these to contact and verify them at delivery. **By registering them you confirm you have the right to share their details with us for this purpose.**
- **Your devices:** a random identifier per installation, and the platform.
- **Check-in:** the rhythm you chose, your last confirmation, and reminder stages.
- **Security log:** events such as a recovery sheet being used or a device being revoked. It contains no key material.
- **Subscription:** your plan and renewal date. Payment happens in your app store; we never see your card.

### If you are an heir

Before release we know only what the vault owner registered, and we do not contact you. At delivery we need your account, the result of your identity check (as above), and how it was matched to your record.

### If you report a death

Your name and how to reach you, your account, the email you entered for the person who died, and the **death certificate** you attach. The certificate is kept without end-to-end encryption because our team reviews it by hand.

### If you write to support

Your messages and their attachments. **Support conversations are not end-to-end encrypted**, and our team reads them. Never send your recovery sheet there — we refuse any message that looks like its code. If you write to us without an account, the name and email you type are not verified.

### If you visit this website

Nothing tracks you before you agree. If you agree, we run Google Analytics to learn how people find the site, with ad personalisation and Google signals switched off. Your choice is stored in your browser, and you can change it from "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page. Analytics runs on this website only — never in the app or the web app.

## Why we process it

- **To provide the service you asked for:** keeping your vault, delivering after death, and reaching you and your heirs.
- **For verification and security:** preventing fraud, making sure we deliver only to the people you named, and telling you what happens on your account.
- **With your consent:** analytics on this website, which you can withdraw at any time.
- **To comply with the law:** where a law in force requires it.

## Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We use providers who process it on our behalf, only as far as needed:

| Provider | Purpose | What it receives |
|---|---|---|
| Clerk | Account sign-in | Name, email and session data |
| Convex | Database and file hosting | Everything above, with vault contents encrypted, and the private half of the delivery key |
| Didit | Identity verification | The document and face images you give it directly |
| Resend | Email | Recipient address and message text |
| Twilio | Text messages to heirs | The heir's number and a message that names no one and nothing |
| Google Analytics | Analytics for this website, with your consent | Browsing data on wassiya.app |
| Apple and Google | Subscriptions through their stores | What payment requires, under their own policies |

## How long we keep it

- **Your vault and heirs:** for as long as your account exists, or until you delete the item or heir.
- **Deliveries:** one year from release; then the whole vault is deleted for good.
- **Support attachments:** deleted 180 days after the conversation is closed.
- **Security log:** kept without edits or deletions, because it is the record of what happened on your account.
- **Death reports and certificates, support text, and emails sent:** [retention periods — to be confirmed].

## Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Law you may know how we process your data, request access to it and a copy of it, have it corrected, have it destroyed once it is no longer needed, and withdraw your consent at any time. You can edit your name, email, country and your heirs' details in the app directly; for anything else, write to [privacy email]. We will reply within the period the law sets.

The only copy of your vault's contents you can read is on your phone; we cannot provide one because we cannot read it.

## Security

We encrypt your vault on your phone, lock what you route to your heirs with our delivery key, and limit our team's access to your data through specific, logged permissions. Details are in [How the encryption works](/en/legal/encryption).
