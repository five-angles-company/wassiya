## Who we are

Wassiya is a service for keeping a digital legacy, operated by [legal entity name and commercial registration number]. For anything about your data: [privacy email].

This policy covers the Wassiya app, wassiya.app, the web app for death reports and executors, and support conversations.

## The principle: your vault is not ours

Your vault's contents are encrypted on your phone before they reach us, and we hold no key to them — not while you live, and not after — so we cannot read them. What follows is what we **can** read, and why we need it. The encryption is explained in [How the encryption works](/en/legal/encryption).

## What we collect

### If you own a vault

- **Your account:** your name, email address, country and language. You sign in with a one-time code; there is no password.
- **Your identity check:** done through a specialist provider (Didit). From the result we keep your verified name, document type, date of birth and verification status. **We keep neither images of your document nor your ID number** — only a keyed, irreversible fingerprint of the number, to match against later.
- **Your vault:** encrypted content we cannot read, plus unencrypted metadata we need to run the service: each item's type, whether it is handed over or private, item count, size, file type, and a reminder date if you set one.
- **Your executors:** the name, phone number and email you register for each executor, a keyed fingerprint of their ID number (required, so that we can match their identity at delivery), and when their sheet was printed. The sheet's code stays on paper; we keep only a locked copy of your release key that we cannot open. We need these details to contact and verify your executors at delivery. **By registering them you confirm you have the right to share their details with us for this purpose.**
- **Your devices:** a random identifier per installation, and the platform.
- **Check-in:** the rhythm you chose, your last confirmation, and reminder stages.
- **Security log:** events such as a recovery sheet being used, an executor sheet being printed or a device being revoked. It contains no key material.
- **Subscription:** your plan and renewal date. Payment happens in your app store; we never see your card.

### If you are an executor

Before release we know only what the vault owner registered, and we do not contact you. At delivery we need your account, the result of your identity check (as above), and how it was matched to your record. The code on your sheet is typed in your own browser and is never sent to us.

### If you report a death

Your name and how to reach you, your account, the email you entered for the person who died, and the **death certificate** you attach. The certificate is kept without end-to-end encryption because our team reviews it by hand. We never ask you to verify your identity, and filing gives you nothing from the vault.

### If you write to support

Your messages and their attachments. **Support conversations are not end-to-end encrypted**, and our team reads them. Never send your recovery sheet or an executor sheet there — we refuse any message that looks like their codes. If you write to us without an account, the name and email you type are not verified.

### If you visit this website

Nothing tracks you before you agree. If you agree, we run Google Analytics to learn how people find the site, with ad personalisation and Google signals switched off. Your choice is stored in your browser, and you can change it from "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page. Analytics runs on this website only — never in the app or the web app.

## Why we process it

- **To provide the service you asked for:** keeping your vault, delivering after death, and reaching you and your executors.
- **For verification and security:** preventing fraud, making sure we deliver only to the executors you named, and telling you what happens on your account.
- **With your consent:** analytics on this website, which you can withdraw at any time.
- **To comply with the law:** where a law in force requires it.

## Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Law you may know how we process your data, request access to it and a copy of it, have it corrected, have it destroyed once it is no longer needed, and withdraw your consent at any time. You can edit your name, email, country and your executors' details in the app directly; for anything else, write to [privacy email]. We will reply within the period the law sets.

The only copy of your vault's contents you can read is on your phone; we cannot provide one because we cannot read it.
